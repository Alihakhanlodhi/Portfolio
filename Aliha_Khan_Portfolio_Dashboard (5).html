PERSONAL BLOG

From Learning to Building: My Journey in Computer Science

By Aliha Khan · Portfolio Journey

My journey in technology has been a gradual process of learning, experimenting and turning ideas into practical work. I am currently studying BS Computer Science, and over time I have developed a strong interest in web development, artificial intelligence, digital tools and problem-solving. My portfolio represents the progress I have made through education, internships and hands-on projects.

Before entering the field of Computer Science, my academic background was in Biology and Pre-Medical. I completed Matric with Biology and scored 789 marks, followed by Intermediate in Pre-Medical with 855 marks. Moving from a medical background into Computer Science became an important turning point for me because it gave me the opportunity to explore technology from a completely new perspective.

Practical experience has played a major role in my learning. I completed multiple internships in different areas, including marketing, technology, front-end development, email marketing, development and artificial intelligence. These experiences helped me understand how professional teams work and gave me exposure to both technical and communication-based roles. My internship journey includes work with NT Engineering and Data Services, Evertyze AI PR, Gaoprift and Developer Hub, along with front-end and paid AI internship experience.

Alongside internships, I worked on a number of projects to improve my technical skills. I created an E-Commerce Application, an iCelo Website Application, a Press Release Application, a RAG project, an SLM project and a To-Do List Application. Each project helped me understand a different part of software development, whether it was interface design, web applications, AI-based workflows, information retrieval or task management.

One of the most important projects for me is this personal portfolio dashboard. I created it to organize my education, skills, internships, projects and professional links in one place. The dashboard is built using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. HTML provides the structure, CSS gives the website its visual design and responsive layout, and JavaScript improves navigation and interaction. I also made the portfolio responsive so it can be viewed on both desktop and mobile devices.

This portfolio is not only a record of what I have already completed; it is also a space that I can continue updating as I learn new technologies, complete new internships and build stronger projects. My goal is to keep improving my technical skills, gain more practical experience and build solutions that are useful, professional and meaningful.

What this portfolio reflects

Continuous learning, adaptability, practical experience, interest in AI and web development, and a growing professional identity in the field of Computer Science.
